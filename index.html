Loopback for Mac

Loopback routes audio seamlessly between applications on your Mac. Create virtual audio devices to capture sound from apps and inputs, then route it precisely where needed. Experience the control of a professional studio mixer, built into your system.

Combine Audio Sources

Easily build virtual devices from multiple applications and physical inputs. Add sources like mics, iTunes, or studio gear to a single table for instant mixing.

System-Wide Availability

Virtual devices appear alongside physical ones in System Settings and any audio app. Select them effortlessly as inputs or outputs across your workflow.

App-to-App Routing

Create pass-thru devices to send audio directly between applications. Set Loopback as output in one app and input in another for fluid, lossless transfer.

Multichannel Power

Configure up to 32 channels with custom mapping. Combine apps, hardware, and nested devices for complex setups like podcasts, recordings, or broadcasts.

Universal Compatibility

Works with any app—from Logic and Ableton to QuickTime—or input device, from USB mics to high-end mixers. Perfect for screencasts, gameplay, and live streams.

How It Works

1. Create a Virtual Device

Open Loopback and add audio sources—apps, mics, or other devices—to your new virtual device.

2. Configure Channels

Adjust mixing, mapping, and nesting as needed. Auto-setup handles most cases; manual controls unlock precision.

3. Route and Use

Select the virtual device in your target app as input or output. Audio flows instantly, system-wide.

4. Monitor and Refine

Watch real-time meters and tweak on the fly. Nested devices scale for advanced chains.
